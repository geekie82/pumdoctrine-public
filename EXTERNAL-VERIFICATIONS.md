# EXTERNAL VERIFICATIONS — the log of anyone outside who checked us
Started 2026-09-22. Rule: entries are appended, never rewritten. An entry requires a
name/handle (or "anonymous"), what they checked, the result, and the evidence pointer.
No entry is invented. An empty log is an honest log.

## FORMAT
| Date | Who | What they checked | Result | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| — | — | (none yet) | — | — |

## WHAT COUNTS
- Running `prove_it.py` / `verify.sh` and reporting the outcome (pass or fail).
- Breaking the sample receipt and showing the failure.
- Auditing the receipt chain, the witness copy, or the checkpoints.
- Finding a defect, an overclaim, or a way to make the record lie.
A failure found by an outsider is the most valuable entry this log can hold.
