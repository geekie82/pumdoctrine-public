**What it is.** pumdoctrine is the governing layer under my AI suite: written laws, a
signed record of everything that happens, and a memory that treats facts as evidence —
so the AI can be trusted because it can be *checked*, not because it sounds confident.

**The laws.** Facts only: every claim is tagged measured, observed, documented — or
unknown. Guessing is forbidden; "I don't know" is a real answer. All rules apply at
once. Corrections are appended, never rewritten. Verify the artefact, not the summary.
Additive only — nothing may restrict. Never prohibit, only empower.

**The record.** Every automated action is signed and chained. Tens of thousands exist.
History is append-only; tampering shows as a break. The whole chain is verified the way
an external auditor would — and known breaks are counted and displayed, never hidden.
Six writers at once can't fork it (tested).

**Memory.** Nothing is true by default. A memory earns promotion — observed, proposed,
reviewed, promoted — with evidence at each step. Contradictions are preserved, not
deleted. Two timelines: when something was true, and when it was recorded. Forgetting
is scheduled; even expiry is an event. Attack tests — poisoning, contradictions,
leakage — measured success: zero.

**Self-healing.** It scans itself, finds faults, repairs them: reversible fixes
automatically, risky ones with backup, irreversible ones need proof or a human. Every
repair is verified end-to-end and recorded; failures roll back. Tripwires narrow its own
permissions. A Goodhart gate halts it when a metric starts gaming itself. One switch
stops everything.

**Self-evolution.** The cycle: research → plan → fact-check → apply → test → fix →
repeat, with a signed receipt every round. You choose how many rounds. Failures are
named and kept — the failure log is the curriculum.

**Awareness.** It keeps a signed self-model: what it is, what it can do, what it can't,
and what it doesn't know. It remembers its own day from its own records. Confidence is
scored against real outcomes; when evidence is thin, it abstains. No consciousness
claims — ever.

**The platform.** pumOFFICE — documents, drive and AI, with the Nebula: your files as a
galaxy of solar systems, no joining lines, game-style flight. pumFORGE, the AI engine.
pumMAIL, a desktop-class mail client. pumSEARCH, a private search engine. And a desktop
console with the full tool deck, governance switches and an upgrade menu.

**Honest limits.** The strongest model tier is offline — the working model carries.
Calibration is measured but not yet at target. Some features are queued, and listed as
such. No external audit yet.

**The promise.** Everyone sells AI that *sounds* smart. This one is built to be
**checked** — its claims sourced, its actions recorded, its corrections visible, its
limits published.
