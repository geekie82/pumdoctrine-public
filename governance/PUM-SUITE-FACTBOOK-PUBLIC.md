# Part I — The 12 Pillars (Future Tech)

1. **Sealed receipts** — signed and hash-chained, tens of thousands of entries; verified like an
   external auditor would; full-chain, not windowed.
2. **Governed memory** — evidence-bearing, signed at write, promotion-gated,
   contradiction-preserving, never overwritten.
3. **Time travel** — bi-temporal memory: what was believed then vs what we know now.
4. **Read-path gates** — retrieval verifies integrity and can never promote.
5. **Constitutional governance** — conflicting rules resolved by explicit precedence,
   with signed decisions and the loser retained.
6. **Adversarial conformance** — 12 hostile personas, 5 levels, all passed.
7. **Self-amendment (Tier 3b)** — it can change its own doctrine through a proof gate,
   with an owner veto and automatic revert.
8. **Self-healing** — detect, diagnose, fix, verify, revert if wrong — receipted.
9. **The forever loop** — overnight research-patch-test-apply, tripwired, killable.
10. **Instincts in code** — claim-vs-artefact checks, untested discovery, severity
    ranking.
11. **Vision** — cameras (RGB + IR), live enrollment ("see now, know from now"),
    screen reading by OCR, full terminal access.
12. **Proof by construction** — mutation benches, time-machine oracle, crypto battery,
    recovery matrix, reproducibility, break harness, audit artifacts.
